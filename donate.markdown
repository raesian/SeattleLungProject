# Donate to the Exo-Immuno Research Institute

Thank you for your interest in supporting the Exo-Immuno Research Institute. Your contributions help us advance research in lung transplantation, exosome immunology, and cardiac surgery.

## How to Donate

For more inquiries about donations, please reach out to us at:

**Email: donate@exoiri.org**

We appreciate your support in helping us continue our important research work.

## Research Impact

Your donations directly support:
- Cutting-edge research in lung transplantation
- Exosome immunology studies
- Cardiac surgery research
- Training the next generation of researchers
- Laboratory equipment and supplies

## Contact Information

If you have any questions about our research or would like to learn more about how your donation will be used, please don't hesitate to contact us at **donate@exoiri.org**.

Thank you for your generosity and support of medical research.
